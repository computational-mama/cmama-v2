## Context
Beyond Bias is a partnership between [Gooey.AI](https://gooey.ai/beyondbias) and [Goethe-Institut India](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/kul/fmd/bia.html) that started in March 2025. It asks a simple question: what happens when artists and communities train image models on their own visual languages, instead of accepting the defaults of big, closed models?

## What happened
- Prompt-a-thons in Bangalore, Delhi and Pune (October 2025), where participants fine-tuned models grounded in their own artistic practice and shared cultural imagination.
- 200+ workshop participants and a consortium of 24 organisations from the culture sector.
- An open [manifesto](https://gooey.ai/beyondbias) on community-led approaches to AI development.
- A workshop at the Royal College of Art, London (February 2026) using the Gooey Image Trainer and Video Generator.
- A research paper: [Beyond Bias: Evaluating Cultural AI Through Participation and Interpretation](https://gooey.ai/research/papers/beyond-bias-evaluating-cultural-ai-through-participation-and-interpretation/) (ICML 2026).

## My part
As part of the Gooey.AI team, I worked on the fine-tuning workflows, including an image model trained on Mughal miniatures.
