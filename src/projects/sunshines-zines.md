[Sun Shines Bright](/projects/sunshinesbright/) is also a series of printed zines. Each one has a QR code on the back that brings you to the [solar archive](https://solararchive.cmama.xyz/zines/), where you can find what is in it and the links it points at. The zines are made with [p5.(gen)zine](https://munusshih.github.io/p5.genzine/), so each one is a small program you can print and fold yourself.

## The zines

- **Vol.1: Experiments in running solar powered servers for Small Language Models** (April 2025). Building a small solar powered server from scratch, and what it takes to self host a language model on renewable power. [Download PDF](https://solararchive.cmama.xyz/zines/pdf/SunShinesBright-Vol1-zine-pagebypage.pdf)
- **Vol.2: Fine-tuning a Small Language Model** (September 2026), with Sameera Mudgal and lekkerying. Making a dataset by hand and using it to steer a small model with LoRA. [Download PDF](https://solararchive.cmama.xyz/zines/pdf/SunShinesBright-Vol2-zine-pagebypage.pdf)
- **Vol.3: Building Solarbot, a portable solar powered AI server** (September 2026), with lekkerying. A Raspberry Pi 5 with a foldable panel and a battery, running speech to text, a small language model and text to speech. [Download PDF](https://solararchive.cmama.xyz/zines/pdf/SunShinesBright-Vol3-zine-pagebypage.pdf)

## Broncode at the KB, The Hague

Sun Shines Bright is part of [Broncode](https://aixdesign.co/projects/broncode), an exhibition and program about AI, books and language by AIxDESIGN and the KB, the National Library of the Netherlands. Books made wholly or partly with AI sit next to artworks that question the technology, with a look at the data, labour and raw materials AI runs on.

- On view: 29 October 2026 to 30 January 2027, free for all
- Place: [KB, The Hague](https://www.kb.nl/agenda/expositie-broncode-boeken-het-ai-tijdperk)
